# Changelog: Kola

Changes to the Kola charts, newest first. Overview of all maps: [CHANGELOG.md](../CHANGELOG.md).

## 2026-11-20: North arrow placement

The north arrow (TN, MN and magnetic variation) now goes to the most open place of the map, away from pavement, taxiways and labels, instead of a fixed corner. Before, it covered part of the map on the aerodrome chart.

- **Changed sheets (2):** `01_GND_BAS100_Vuojarvi_P1`, `01_PAR_BAS100_Vuojarvi_P2`. File names are unchanged.
- The other Kola sheets and the other maps follow with their next update.
